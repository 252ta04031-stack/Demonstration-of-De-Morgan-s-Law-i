# Demonstration-of-De-Morgan-s-Law-i
De Morgan's Law, a fundamental rule in Boolean algebra and digital logic. It compares the results of !(A &amp;&amp; B) with (!A || !B) and !(A || B) with (!A &amp;&amp; !B) to prove they are logically equivalent. The program helps students understand logical operators, conditional statements, and their applications in programming and digital circuit design.
